All you need to know about Data Flow Diagrams
What are data flow
Diagrams?

Data Flow Diagrams are the heart of system layout, they consist of a bunch of arrows and shapes, all building up together to create a hollistic view of your system. Many businesses can find it essential to use DFD’s since it can be seen as a universal language that can be understood by engineers and non-engineers.

History Of DFD

Edward Nash Yourdon was a developer of structured anaylsis techniques in the 1970s and one of the main people who was involved in the upbringing of Data Flow Diagrams. The structured design concept had taken off in the software engineering field and Data flow diagrams had taken off with it.

Who are they for?

You may wonder who are Data Flow Diagrams for. They can be very useful to people that are in development of a new system or a ongoing system which you may want to apply new ideas to. By having a DFD in your arsenal you can have many benefits coming your way.
